Sweets from every kitchen we love

Bakerz & Bitez began with one oven and a simple promise: every sweet should feel like a small celebration. We bake French pastry with real butter and Madagascan vanilla, stir our mithai the slow way with ghee and cardamom, and borrow the best desserts from every corner of the world.

Cakes are baked to order, mithai is made fresh each afternoon, and the ice cream is swirled or scooped while you wait. Where smiles are served daily is not a slogan. It is the job.

Questions, answered

❦
Do you deliver?

Yes. Choose delivery at checkout. The fee is shown where available; when it needs confirmation, the bakery will confirm it with your time slot.

How far ahead should I order a cake?

48 hours for gâteaux and 5 days for wedding cakes. For same-day orders, call us and we will see what we can do.

Do you have options for allergies?

Every product is made in a kitchen that handles nuts, gluten, eggs and dairy. Please talk to us before ordering if you have an allergy.

Can I choose the flavours in a gift box?

Yes. Use the box builder to pick every macaron, then add a gift message at checkout.

How long do macarons keep?

Up to five days in the fridge. Take them out 20 minutes before eating for the best texture.
